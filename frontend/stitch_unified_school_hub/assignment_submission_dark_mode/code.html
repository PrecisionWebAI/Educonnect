Ecosystems Research Project

100 points

description Instructions

Research a local ecosystem and prepare a detailed report covering the following aspects:

  • Identify at least 5 native plant species and 5 native animal species.
  • Describe the food web dynamics within this ecosystem.
  • Discuss any current environmental threats (e.g., pollution, invasive species) affecting this area.
  • Propose realistic conservation efforts.

Your report should be 3-5 pages long, double-spaced. Please include at least one diagram or chart.
