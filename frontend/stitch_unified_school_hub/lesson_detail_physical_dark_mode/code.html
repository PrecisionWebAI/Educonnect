I'm slightly confused about the difference between the rough and smooth endoplasmic reticulum. Can someone clarify?

school
Mr. Davis (Teacher)

Great question, Alex! The rough ER has ribosomes attached to its surface (making it look 'rough'), which are involved in protein synthesis. The smooth ER lacks these ribosomes and is mainly involved in lipid synthesis.
