In this lesson, we dive deep into the fundamental unit of life: the cell. We will explore the differences between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, and examine the structure and function of key organelles.

Key Concepts Covered:

  • The Cell Theory and its history.
  • Detailed structure of the cell membrane (Phospholipid bilayer).
  • Functions of major organelles: Nucleus, Mitochondria, Ribosomes, and Endoplasmic Reticulum.
  • Differences between plant and animal cells.
