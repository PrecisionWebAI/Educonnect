Ecosystems Research Project

Instructions

For this project, you will need to research a specific ecosystem of your choice and present your findings.

  • Identify the producers, consumers, and decomposers within your chosen ecosystem.
  • Create a detailed food web diagram showing the flow of energy.
  • Discuss the impact of human activities on this ecosystem and propose potential conservation efforts.
  • Your final submission should be a minimum of 3 pages (excluding diagrams).
