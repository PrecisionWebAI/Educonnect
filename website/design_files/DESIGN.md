## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, pristine, and luminous operating atmosphere tailored for next-generation educational leadership and campus operations. Moving away from standard educational software tropes that skew either juvenile or heavily bureaucratic, this aesthetic communicates executive precision, architectural transparency, and technological intelligence.

The visual style blends refined **Glassmorphism** with crisp **Modern Corporate** engineering:
- **Spatial Translucency:** Structural cards, navigation docks, and metric surfaces exist as polished layers of optical-grade frosted glass, creating an open spatial continuum rather than dense structural walls.
- **Luminous Radiance:** The interface channels refracted light through ambient mesh spills, giving digital workspaces the feeling of sunlit physical architecture.
- **Institutional Weight:** To preserve legibility and institutional authority, high-contrast deep slate and navy tones anchor all typographic hierarchies against the luminous background, ensuring accessibility and trust.

## Colors

The palette simulates sunlight interacting with crystalline glass architecture:

- **Primary (`#2563EB` - Electric Royal Blue):** The primary brand anchor for essential CTAs, active operational toggles, real-time sync states, and primary focal points.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Vibrant Cyan):** Represents connectivity, edge hardware feeds, network health, and high-priority visual accents.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** Reserved for system analytics, machine intelligence indicators, and deep operational insights.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Slate Navy):** The structural anchor ensuring high-contrast legibility across translucent panels.
- **Canvas & Gradients:** Base canvas flows between `#F8FAFF` and `#EEF2FF`, warmed by diffuse ambient mesh blurs of `#38BDF8`, `#6366F1`, and `#2563EB` set at 8% to 14% opacity.
- **Glass Tokens:** Light glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`) handles structural overlays and data cards; dense glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) ensures legibility for modals, persistent navigation, and critical metric tables.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Manrope**'s geometric clarity with **Inter**'s neutral, utilitarian discipline.

- **Display & Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric precision engineered with slightly tightened tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to project architectural structure and executive confidence across marketing hero statements, metric banners, and section titles.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** System-grade optical legibility ensures that dense campus data grids, schedule streams, and system dialogues remain readable even when rendered over translucent, blurred surfaces.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** All display and major section titles utilize high-contrast Slate Navy (`#0F172A`). Long-form text uses Slate (`#475569`) to reduce eye strain against high-luminance frosted backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured **Fluid Grid** engineered on an 8pt architectural rhythm, calibrated to give floating glass surfaces room to breathe.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (`max-w-7xl` centered), transitioning to 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop uses wide outer framing (`margin: 3rem`) paired with generous internal card gutters (`gutter: 1.5rem`) to maintain separation between translucent glass edges and prevent visual blur collision. Mobile collapses margins to `1.25rem` to maximize spatial utility.
- **Stacking Spacing:** Vertical composition relies heavily on `space-xl` and higher multiples (`3rem` to `5rem` between distinct campus modular blocks), allowing the luminous canvas glow to shine through as natural layout dividers instead of solid horizontal lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through optical depth, physical blur levels, specular border refraction, and luminous chromatic ambient shadows:

1. **Level 0 (Canvas):**
   - Ethereal luminous gradient canvas (`#F8FAFF` to `#EEF2FF`) infused with low-frequency radial blurs (`filter: blur(80px)`) in Ice Blue (`#38BDF8`), Royal Blue (`#2563EB`), and Indigo (`#6366F1`) at 8–12% opacity.

2. **Level 1 (Ground Glass Panels & Content Cards):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` top/left with fallback edge `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

3. **Level 2 (Floating Nav Docks, Tooltips, Modals):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px) saturate(200%)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`.
   - Shadow: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`.

4. **Interactive State (Hover & Active Focus):**
   - When users hover over panels, an internal specular gradient overlay activates (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.4) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.0) 100%)`) accompanied by a subtle 2px upward translation and cyan-tinted light bloom (`0 12px 36px -6px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.16)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature to deliver an aerodynamic, human-centric, and contemporary physical OS atmosphere:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form elements, standard controls, input fields, and inline status badges.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Applied to primary cards, modal dialogues, floating toolbars, and dynamic dashboards.
- **Hero & Shell Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Applied to full-page app frames, prominent hero feature showcases, and interactive hardware monitor windows.
- **Continuous Curvature:** All rounded corners maintain smooth G2-continuity to echo tactile, milled glass hardware interfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Electric Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) with subtle inner specular highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), rounded to `0.5rem`. On hover, color transitions to `#1D4ED8` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Translucent frosted pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), backdrop blur of 12px, 1px border stroke (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`), text `#0F172A`. Hover triggers light background bloom (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless, Slate Navy text (`#1E293B`), active on subtle cyan underglow or icon hover transition.

### Cards & Glass Panels
- Built using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-blur-md` (16px), bordered by a multi-stop pseudo-stroke simulating light incidence: 1px top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` grading down to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.5)`.
- Internal padding matches `space-lg` (`1.5rem`). Content inside cards must never rest directly against the glass border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input backgrounds feature soft recessed glass (`rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.6)`) with an inset shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`).
- Active/Focus state shifts background to solid pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px ring in Electric Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) and a soft cyan ambient spill (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.18)`).

### Chips & Badges
- Dynamic status indicators use tinted glass micro-pills (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`) with `1px` matching color borders (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`), text in `#2563EB`, paired with a pulsating 6px dot for real-time telemetry (e.g., "Campus OS v4.2 Active").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box dimensions: 18px × 18px, rounded to 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Default: Translucent white with slate outline (`rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.8)`). Checked: Electric Royal Blue fill (`#2563EB`) with a pure white SVG icon, casting a localized blue micro-glow.

### OS-Specific Elements (Status Ribbon & Floating Dock)
- **Top Bar System Monitor:** A slim persistent frosted ribbon at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` housing real-time network, campus facility health, and automated agent status indicators.
- **Floating Command Dock:** Fixed bottom floating island with heavy frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), `backdrop-blur-xl`, providing rapid operational shortcuts with icon hover scale effects (`scale-110` with cyan specular reflection).
